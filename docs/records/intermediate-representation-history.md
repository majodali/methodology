# Intermediate representation specification: history

This record keeps the history of how the intermediate-representation specification came to be, with the decisions behind each change. It sits outside the reviewed text, so a stranger who reviews the specification does not read it.

Status: record. Moved out of the [specification](../intermediate-representation.md) and [its IR](../intermediate-representation.ir.md) on 2026-11-02, at count 7, by communication-pipeline [evaluation case 015](https://github.com/majodali/communication-pipeline/blob/main/eval/cases/015-ruleset-simplification-review-2026-11-02.md) change 20 (the history leaves the reviewed text).
